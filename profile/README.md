# 👋 Welcome to 180 Degrees Consulting, Arequipa 🏔️

<p align="left"><strong>Student led consulting for social impact.</strong> 🌍</p>

<p align="left">
  <strong>180 Degrees Consulting Arequipa</strong> is a consulting branch led by students, founded in <strong>2026</strong> at the Universidad Nacional de San Agustín de Arequipa (UNSA) 🎓. We are part of the global <a href="https://180dc.org/">180 Degrees Consulting</a> network, the world's largest student driven consultancy for nonprofits and social enterprises. Our purpose is to connect university talent with organizations working to create positive change, supporting social enterprises, nonprofits, and initiatives with practical and sustainable solutions to their challenges.
</p>

<p align="left">
  We believe meaningful impact comes from both the solutions we deliver and the people behind them ✨. Our mission is to empower students to create lasting social impact by bridging academic knowledge with real world application, helping organizations grow while developing the next generation of consultants and leaders in Arequipa.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <img src="images/Fotografia%20TICS.jpg" alt="TICS Team, 180DC Arequipa" height="350">
</p>

<p align="left">
  Born within UNSA, our branch brings together students from different backgrounds, disciplines, and areas of expertise 👥. From technology and digital transformation to strategy, operations, marketing, and data analytics, our multidisciplinary teams work collaboratively with organizations to understand their context and deliver actionable recommendations tailored to their needs.
</p>

<p align="left">
  Beyond consulting, 180DC Arequipa is a platform for learning and leadership 🚀. Our members gain hands on experience through real projects, strengthen their problem solving and communication skills, and grow as professionals while contributing directly to the development of our community.
</p>
