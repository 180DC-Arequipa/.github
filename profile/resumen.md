180 Degrees Consulting – Arequipa

Student-led consulting for social impact.

180 Degrees Consulting Arequipa is a student-led consulting branch founded in 2026 by students from the Universidad Nacional de San Agustín de Arequipa (UNSA). We are part of the global 180 Degrees Consulting network, bringing together motivated students and young professionals who want to use their knowledge, skills, and creativity to address real-world challenges.

Our branch was created with a simple purpose: to connect university talent with organizations working to create positive change in society. Through consulting projects, we support social enterprises, nonprofits, initiatives, and purpose-driven organizations in developing practical and sustainable solutions to their challenges.

At the same time, we believe that meaningful impact also comes from developing the people behind it. 180DC Arequipa provides students with the opportunity to work on real consulting challenges, collaborate across disciplines, develop professional skills, and grow as leaders while contributing to the development of our community.

Our Mission

To empower students to create meaningful social impact by providing high-quality consulting support to organizations and initiatives addressing real-world challenges.

We aim to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world application, transforming student talent into practical solutions that create lasting value for organizations and their communities.

Our Vision

To become a leading student-driven consulting organization in Arequipa, recognized for developing exceptional young leaders and creating meaningful, sustainable impact through collaboration, innovation, and knowledge.

We aspire to build a strong community where talent, purpose, and action come together to contribute to a better future for Arequipa and beyond.

Our consulting teams work alongside organizations to understand their challenges, identify opportunities, and develop actionable recommendations.

Strategy & Growth

Our approach is collaborative and impact-oriented. We seek to understand each organization's context before developing solutions that are practical, sustainable, and tailored to their needs.
